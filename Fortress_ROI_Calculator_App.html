2 Installation Advantage labor Fortress avoids, added savings ✓

Because Fortress is a prefabricated stainless steel wall, it consolidates construction timelines and eliminates on-site finish labor. This uses the days saved from the Installation Advantage timeline, converted to labor hours (days × laborers × 8 hrs), then priced at the state rate. The full amount is treated as an additional saving that reduces the True Delta on the ROI page.

The complete-assembly cost ranges are directional benchmarks compiled from the references below, intended as a starting point for a true-cost estimate, not a final estimating database. Several are manufacturer, distributor, or general-market references. For a customer-facing quote, complete operating-room wall costs should be validated with RSMeans, local subcontractor bids, and recent healthcare-project estimates.

Altro: Operating Rooms Supports the use of hygienic, smooth, cleanable wall cladding systems such as Altro Whiterock in OR environments. Product/application context; not a construction-cost database.
HomeGuide: Drywall Installation Cost General-market installed drywall cost ranges. Baseline component reference only; does not represent a complete healthcare partition or OR installation.
GTO FRP: FRP Panel Installation Cost Guide Directional FRP material and installation pricing. Manufacturer/vendor source; excludes the full wall assembly and hospital-specific conditions.
Koffler Sales: Heavy-Duty Commercial Vinyl Cost Guide Directional commercial vinyl pricing and welding context. Primarily flooring-focused, so not direct evidence of installed wall-covering cost.
Grainger: Acrovyn PETG Wall Covering (0.05") Retail product listing for a material-only price point. Excludes adhesive, trims, freight, waste, substrate prep, and labor.
Grainger: Acrovyn PETG Wall Covering (0.06") Second listing showing how thickness/specification changes sheet price. Excludes all installation-related costs.
RSMeans Data Online Best source here for formalizing labor, material, equipment, crew productivity, and regional adjustments. Detailed data generally requires a subscription.
The Spruce: Tile Installation Cost General-market tile installation range, used directionally. Not healthcare-specific; excludes epoxy grout, infection-control measures, specialty backing, and renovation premiums.

Methodology. Ranges reflect complete traditional wall assembly (finish material + substrate + installation labor), shown as low–high rather than a single universal cost. Lost OR revenue is kept as a separate, user-controlled input on the ROI tab. Validate assumptions with RSMeans national baselines, at least two regional subcontractor bids, and by separating new-construction from occupied-hospital renovation pricing.
